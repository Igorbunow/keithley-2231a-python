## Evidence boundaries — do not overclaim

1. `0.207 s` is **one observed idle-response interval**, not a worst-case
   response time, benchmark, precision measurement or proof of completion
   ordering when the instrument has queued work. The 3 s transport timeout
   was configured; timeout behavior was not physically triggered.
2. Zero ESER/SRER before and after means the enable masks were unchanged
   *at their zero setting*. The test never reads or consumes SESR/`*ESR?`,
   so it cannot claim OPC/event-latch preservation, IRQ/SRQ functionality or
   status bit behavior.
3. OFF was observed in consecutive channel readbacks, **not** continuously
   or using an independent DMM. No load, DUT, oscilloscope, KEL103 or meter
   was connected or sampled. No electrical outputs were measured.
4. The final `INST:NSEL 1` write was error-polled, but not independently
   re-read with `INST:NSEL?` afterward. The last `SYST:LOC` intentionally has
   no error poll; the physical OFF state after LOCAL was operator-confirmed.
5. BENCH/INITIAL operator keystrokes occurred before opening evidence,
   so JSONL's `pre_open_approvals` marker is retrospective, not the actual
   instant of their confirmation. `dirty=true` is not normalized into a
   false clean-tree assertion.
6. No evidence justifies enabling `*WAI`, `*OPC` writes, pending-operation
   experiments, SCPI triggers or hazardous hardware commands.
