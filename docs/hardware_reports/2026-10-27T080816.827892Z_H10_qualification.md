## Qualification limits and evidence

This proves aggregate operation only through 0.300 A and below 1 W. It does not prove current sharing between CH1 and CH2; no branch-current instruments were used. The separate proposed 4.00 A / 3.00 V / CR 0.50 Ω (about 2 V / 8 W) point remains pending. Parallel mode disables the observed hardware voltage limiter, so do not treat the software cap as hardware OVP.
