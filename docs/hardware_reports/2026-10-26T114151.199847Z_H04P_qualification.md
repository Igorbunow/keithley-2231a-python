direct SCPI over-limit probe was performed on CH1–CH3 with outputs OFF. Each

performed the panel attempts on all three channels; independent readbacks were
4.50 V / 0.100 A and the runner restored 3.30 V on each channel with a clear queue.

incorrectly treated this transient status as output OFF and shut the channel down

runner now waits up to 3 s for the OPER ON bit while verifying selected output ON

OFF after cleanup. H04P remains incomplete and is not HW VERIFIED.

identity query; the runner does not pin behavior to firmware version.
