bit 3 in the operation summary. The CV function is supported by the panel and electrical measurements. The runner's
`FAIL` outcome records its status guard, not a CV functional failure or an SCPI error;
all error-queue replies were zero. The meaning/source of the status responses still needs
diagnosis. Keep the raw status values and `UNKNOWN` decode; do not substitute one register
for another. H03 remains PARTIAL; CH3 CC has not run.
